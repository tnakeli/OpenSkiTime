Stay with the race.

Follow starters, racers on course, impulses and ranking together. Preserve raw timing input and the history of corrections.

03 / PUBLISH

Bring everyone along.

Share unofficial live results in a browser, locally or online. Optional publishing runs independently of timing capture.
