AUTOMATION & INTEGRATIONS

Less repeated work.

Bring race data and timing sources into the same workflow. Review imports and important decisions before saving.

FIS DATA

Start with existing information.

Preview calendar and competition data, look up homologations, and update athletes from a FIS points list. Prepare the data online, then use saved race information and downloaded points lists offline.

EXCEL & REGISTRATION

Enter once. Review together.

Paste competitor rows from Excel, review proposed changes and save them as a batch. Reuse category rules and manage participation across competitions.

ALGE TIMING SOURCES

Connect your timing workflow.

Use supported Timy USB, MT1 serial or ALGE Results sources. Follow captured impulses, elapsed times and ranking without switching between separate tools.

RESULT CALCULATIONS

Let the calculations follow.

Calculate run times, rankings, race points and the FIS penalty from saved inputs. Review results with the TD and export approved XML. FIS submission is currently test-only.

LOCAL RECEIPT RECOGNITION

Spend less time transcribing.

Read printed timestamps from receipt images locally. Review proposed B and hand-clock matches against A timing, then confirm the values to use. Original images remain available as evidence.

LIVE RESULTS

Keep viewers up to date.

Publish unofficial results locally or to a cloud server. The browser receives updates automatically; timing capture continues independently if the online connection fails.

You control the workflow: imports and recognized timing evidence require review. Online integrations are optional; local race operation works offline.
