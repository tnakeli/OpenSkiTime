# Active competition in the workspace

The title bar shows OpenSkiTime, the active competition's short label and run (for example `SL Women / Run 1`) and its full competition name in every workspace view. Long names are shortened with an ellipsis when space is limited; hovering shows the complete name. The competition and run are chosen in the **Start lists**, **Timing**, **Results**, **Timing report** and **PDF Factory** menus; the title bar only displays them.

On Windows the navy bar is the window's title bar: drag its empty area to move the window, double-click to maximize or restore, and Windows' own minimize, maximize and close buttons remain at the right. **File** holds New series, Open file, Open recent, Backup / transfer and Close file. **Settings** opens the settings view and its menu goes directly to a settings tab.

Selecting a start-list run, timing run or results competition updates the shared competition context. Moving to Event series, Competitions, Competitors or Settings retains the active competition. Editing its name updates the shared heading when the saved series is reloaded. Closing the series clears the selection and name.

The window title displays the open series file path, or OpenSkiTime when no file is open. Start lists and Timing do not repeat the competition/run/codex heading inside their content; operational state and draw help remain visible, and the timing summary (starters, on course, finished) is at the right of the status bar while Timing is open.

Read-only competition and calendar date columns display `dd.MM.yyyy` without writing their formatted text back to date models. Rendering under an English or Finnish system culture must neither change a race date nor trigger date-conversion feedback. Date edits go through the reviewed competition/series forms.
