## Limits

Linux execution and its default-reader integration were not run. Physical timing hardware and FIS acceptance of the generated form were not tested. Official results and penalty PDFs inherit the existing standard one/two-run FIS engines and reviewed 2026/27 rule tables; unsupported formats remain unavailable. The Timing Report PDF is a local metadata/evidence summary, separate from approved FIS timing XML. Large referee tables and long DSQ notes can paginate the fixed-margin form.

The new print-profile Preview command was tested through an injected PDF-opener boundary and by PDF parsing; its launch in a real default PDF reader was not manually repeated. A reader that locks a temporary preview can prevent cleanup when the application closes.
